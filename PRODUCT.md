# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Shipping operations staff, reviewers, and team leads evaluating or using SDOC to classify inbox traffic, compare Shipping Instructions with draft Bills of Lading, and resolve exceptions with source evidence.

## Product Purpose

SDOC turns shipping-document checks into a clear, traceable workflow. The landing page should quickly communicate that the system finds the right requests, verifies seven shipment fields, exposes discrepancies, and routes uncertainty to a person instead of guessing.

## Brand Personality

Assured, operational, and precise. The experience should feel calm under pressure, technically credible, and accountable without becoming cold or overly corporate.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS styling, dense clusters of tiny labels, inflated claims, decorative metrics, excessive glass effects, and interfaces that imply autonomous decisions without human accountability.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the operational outcome, then reveal the mechanism.
2. Make evidence and accountability visible, not merely claimed.
3. Use hierarchy and spacing to communicate confidence.
4. Preserve shipping-domain specificity in copy and demonstrations.
5. Keep the path from discrepancy to human action obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior. Never rely on color alone to communicate a verification state.
