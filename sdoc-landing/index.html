From inbox to decision in four steps

One workflow from the incoming request to a decision your team can act on.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Classify each message by intent, not just its subject line, so SDOC knows whether a comparison is needed.

  2. 02

    Extract

    Read the attached SI and BL, pull out the seven critical fields, and keep each value tied to its source.

  3. 03

    Compare

    Normalise formats and units, then compare field by field with business rules, not a model's guess.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    Route anything uncertain to a person with full context. Every decision is recorded.
